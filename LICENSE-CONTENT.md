## Textbooks are not included
The Iranian and Afghan school textbooks are **copyrighted** by their publishers, and they are **not** in this repository.
- `curriculum/` and `curriculum_af/` record only their tables of contents: chapter and lesson titles. These are facts about the curriculum, used to align topics.
- The download scripts fetch the books for local reference only.
- All Kamangir lesson content is original. **Contributions must not copy text, exercises or images from the textbooks.**
